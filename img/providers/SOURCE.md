# Provider icons

The icons of OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini, Mistral, Ollama, DeepSeek, OpenRouter and LM Studio are from Simple Icons (https://simpleicons.org, CC0 1.0). The names and marks belong to their owners; Totem shows them only to identify the provider that a person has chosen. The icons of Groq, llama.cpp, Together AI, xAI, Fireworks, Cerebras, Hugging Face, NVIDIA, Moonshot, vLLM and Cohere are from LobeHub Icons (https://lobehub.com/icons, https://github.com/lobehub/lobe-icons), MIT License, Copyright (c) 2023 LobeHub. Their marks belong to their owners and are used in the same way. The icon of LiteLLM was taken from https://www.litellm.ai. The originals are in `assets/providers/`; `node scripts/normalize-icons.js` turns them into the files of this folder: the same box (24 by 24), the drawing fitted in 20 by 20 and centered, one colour (`currentColor`), no size and no style. A provider without an icon shows its initial.
